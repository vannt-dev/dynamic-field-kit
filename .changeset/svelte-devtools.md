`DynamicFormDevTools` for Svelte, the floating overlay the React, Vue and Angular adapters already have: data, errors, meta (dirty and touched) and fields tabs, a red badge with the number of fields in error on the collapsed button, and `position` (`bottom-right` or `bottom-left`). It takes the same props as the others (`data`, `errors`, `touched`, `isDirty`, `fields`, `position`), or a form from `createDynamicForm` through `form`, with a prop passed next to it winning.
